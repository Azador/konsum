Konsum is a shopping list that several people use together – your family, flatmates or club. It works offline and syncs as soon as you are connected again.

The app is currently available in German only.

Features
• Several groups, each with as many lists as you like
• Add items quickly: suggestions by frequency, quantity and unit
• Categories and shops with their own order – the list follows your way through the store
• Barcode scanner to add and check off items
• Product data and nutrition facts from Open Food Facts on request
• Shared and private notes per entry
• Prices with history and an estimated total for the list
• Recurring items can be added automatically, with a quantity based on past consumption
• Works completely offline

Sharing via Matrix
Konsum runs no server of its own. Each group is a room on a Matrix server of your choice, for example matrix.org or your club's server. Lists and shared notes are end-to-end encrypted. Without signing in, everything stays on your device.

Privacy
No ads, no tracking, no Google services. The developers receive no data from you.

Konsum is free software (GPL-3.0). Source code, bug reports and wishes: https://github.com/Azador/konsum
